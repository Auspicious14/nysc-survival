Characters and animations: Mixamo ("Lewis" with Walking, Idle, Running, Talking; female character "Ch26" with Walking, Idle, Running, Talking; the Samba dance and Xbot clips from the three.js examples are retargeted offline). Outfits are recoloured offline (tools/bake.js).
Second male character: Mixamo "Ch41" with Walking, Idle, Running, Standing Arguing, Texting While Standing, Typing, Waving, Sitting Idle.
Music: royalty-free afrobeat tracks from Pixabay (Pixabay Content License) by FreeMusicForVideo ("Afrobeat Afro Beat" #456650), PlayStarz_Music ("Afrobeat Afro Beat" #496406) and Kiravale ("Afrobeat" #593655).
Voices: generated with YarnGPT (Nigerian-accented open TTS).
Sky: three.js Sky shader. Buildings, roads, signs and street furniture: procedural, made for this game.
Shop names and the "Local Govt. Secretariat" are fictional. Not an official NYSC product.

Nigeria data (js/naija.js): state names, capitals, official nicknames and the 774 LGA names from the ng.states dataset by skaex (github.com/skaex/ng.states). Orientation camp locations from public listings (legit.ng, 2025); camps can move, so treat them as indicative. Urban/rural tags, costs, foods, greetings, organisations and climate notes are game design, not official data. All organisations and people in the game are fictional.
Phone games: "Whot!" follows the common Nigerian Whot rules (Hold on, Pick two, Suspension, General market, Whot 20). Danfo Dash is an original mini-game.

Real streets, buildings, place names and landmark positions: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL), openstreetmap.org/copyright. Extracted from the Geofabrik Nigeria file with osmium (tools/NYSC_OSM_Extract.ipynb, tools/osm_prep.py). Data in osm/*.json is ODbL.
