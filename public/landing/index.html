Chapter one · Orientation camp

Twenty-one days in khaki.

A soldier at the gate wants your call-up letter. Your bag is next. That kettle? He will pretend he didn't see it.

Thumbprint, passport photos, kit. Then the parade ground: “Platoon 5… attention!” Right turn, left turn, about turn, until the bugle says otherwise.

Twenty-one days later, your posting: a new LGA, a new street, a PPA you have never heard of, and a danfo conductor shouting your bus stop.

Questions

What is Kopa Life?

Kopa Life is a free 3D game about the Nigerian NYSC service year. You get a call-up letter, go through orientation camp, get posted to a real street in your state, work at your PPA, attend CDS, queue for monthly clearance and finish at POP. It plays in your browser on phones and computers.

Is Kopa Life an official NYSC game?

No. Kopa Life is a fictional game made by a serving corper. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Youth Service Corps, and the characters in it are made up.

Is it free? Do I need to download anything?

It is free and there is nothing to download. Open kopalife.com.ng in your browser and press Play. You can also add it to your home screen like an app.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It is built for phones first, including budget Androids. On slower phones the game switches to Lite graphics automatically so it stays smooth.

Can I play in my own state?

Yes. All 36 states and the FCT are in the game, each with its own orientation camp, LGAs and real streets built from OpenStreetMap data. Pick your state on the call-up letter, or let the game pick for you.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes. Players posted to the same state camp, LGA or PPA meet online. You can wave, chat, send stickers and private messages. Share your invite link and your friends start in your state.

Is my data safe?

Your progress is saved on your own device. The game only counts anonymous players per state so we know how many people are playing; it does not ask for your name, email or phone number.
