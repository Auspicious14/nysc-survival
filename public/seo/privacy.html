Privacy

Kopa Life is a free, fictional game about the NYSC service year, made by an independent developer. It is not affiliated with the National Youth Service Corps. We collect as little as we can.

What we do not collect

  • Your real name, email, phone number, photos or contacts.
  • Your location from GPS. The state you play in is the one you choose in the game.
  • No ads, no tracking pixels, and we never sell or share data for marketing.

What stays on your phone

Your saved game, your settings and your corper name are kept in your browser's storage on your device. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes them.

Playing online

When you play online, other players in your camp, LGA or PPA can see your corper name, outfit, where you are standing, and the messages you send to everyone. Those messages pass through our realtime service and are not stored. Private messages are encrypted end to end: only you and the person you write to can read them. Phone numbers and links are hidden in chat to protect players from scams. Never share bank details or your home address with someone you met in a game.

Online chat is meant for people aged 18 and over.

Who helps us run the game

Vercel hosts the website and Supabase runs the database and online play. Map data comes from OpenStreetMap. They process data only to provide these services.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Because we do not know who you are, tell us the date and the state you played in, and we will find and delete the matching records.

Contact

For privacy questions or to report a security problem, write to us through our private reporting page. Please do not test the security of the game in a way that affects other players.
