The Jigawa orientation camp

Corpers deployed to Jigawa State report to the orientation camp at Fanisau, Dutse LGA for about three weeks of registration, kitting, swearing-in, parade, SAED lectures and evenings at the mammy market. At the end of camp, everyone gets a posting letter to a PPA in one of the state's LGAs.

Camp locations can change between batches, for example when a camp is being renovated or for security reasons. Always confirm with your call-up letter and the official NYSC portal before you travel.

In Kopa Life your service year starts at the camp gate too. A soldier checks your call-up letter, you queue for registration, collect your khaki and jungle boots, and learn drills with your platoon. Our NYSC camp guide has the full packing list and daily routine.

Corper life in Dutse

The weather is hot days and dusty harmattan mornings. To get around, corpers use keke (tricycles), okada (motorbikes) and buses. In the game, food and transport prices in Jigawa are among the cheapest in the game.

Local languages you will hear: Hausa. Expect greetings like “Sannu, corper!” and “Ina kwana?”. At the buka you will find Tuwo shinkafa & miyan kuka, Masa, Suya and Kosai, with Kunu to wash it down.
