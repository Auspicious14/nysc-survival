The Bayelsa orientation camp

Corpers deployed to Bayelsa State report to the orientation camp at Kaiama, Kolokuma/Opokuma LGA for about three weeks of registration, kitting, swearing-in, parade, SAED lectures and evenings at the mammy market. At the end of camp, everyone gets a posting letter to a PPA in one of the state's LGAs.

Camp locations can change between batches, for example when a camp is being renovated or for security reasons. Always confirm with your call-up letter and the official NYSC portal before you travel.

In Kopa Life your service year starts at the camp gate too. A soldier checks your call-up letter, you queue for registration, collect your khaki and jungle boots, and learn drills with your platoon. Our NYSC camp guide has the full packing list and daily routine.

LGAs in Bayelsa you can be posted to

Bayelsa State has 8 local government areas. Your posting letter names one of them, and that LGA's NYSC office is where you do monthly clearance and meet your LGI. Busier, more urban areas are outlined in green.

  • Brass
  • Ekeremor
  • Kolokuma/Opokuma
  • Nembe
  • Ogbia
  • Sagbama
  • Southern Ijaw
  • Yenagoa

1 of 8 is a busier urban area in the game: more corpers, longer clearance queues and a louder group chat.

Corper life in Yenagoa

The weather is humid, wet and surrounded by water. To get around, corpers use keke (tricycles), okada (motorbikes) and buses. In the game, food and transport prices in Bayelsa are among the cheapest in the game.

Rivers, creeks and boats. Some PPAs are a boat ride away.

Local languages you will hear: Pidgin. Expect greetings like “How far, corper?” and “Abeg welcome!”. At the buka you will find Banga soup & starch, Bole & fish, Afang soup & eba and Edikaikong, with Chapman to wash it down.
