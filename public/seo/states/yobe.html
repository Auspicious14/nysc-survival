Serving in Yobe State: camp, LGAs and corper life

The Yobe orientation camp

Corpers deployed to Yobe State report to the orientation camp at Dazigau, Nangere LGA (Potiskum–Kano Road) for about three weeks of registration, kitting, swearing-in, parade, SAED lectures and evenings at the mammy market. At the end of camp, everyone gets a posting letter to a PPA in one of the state's LGAs.

Camp locations can change between batches, for example when a camp is being renovated or for security reasons. Always confirm with your call-up letter and the official NYSC portal before you travel.

In Kopa Life your service year starts at the camp gate too. A soldier checks your call-up letter, you queue for registration, collect your khaki and jungle boots, and learn drills with your platoon. Our NYSC camp guide has the full packing list and daily routine.

LGAs in Yobe you can be posted to

Yobe State has 17 local government areas. Your posting letter names one of them, and that LGA's NYSC office is where you do monthly clearance and meet your LGI. Busier, more urban areas are outlined in green.

1 of 17 is a busier urban area in the game: more corpers, longer clearance queues and a louder group chat.

Corper life in Damaturu

The weather is hot days and dusty harmattan mornings. To get around, corpers use keke (tricycles), okada (motorbikes) and buses. In the game, food and transport prices in Yobe are among the cheapest in the game.

Local languages you will hear: Hausa / Kanuri / Fulfulde. Expect greetings like “Sannu da zuwa, corper!” and “Ina kwana?”. At the buka you will find Tuwo masara & miyan kuka, Masa & miyan taushe, Kilishi and Fura da nono, with Fura da nono to wash it down.
