The Nasarawa orientation camp

Corpers deployed to Nasarawa State report to the orientation camp at Keffi (Magaji Dan-Yamusa camp) for about three weeks of registration, kitting, swearing-in, parade, SAED lectures and evenings at the mammy market. At the end of camp, everyone gets a posting letter to a PPA in one of the state's LGAs.

Camp locations can change between batches, for example when a camp is being renovated or for security reasons. Always confirm with your call-up letter and the official NYSC portal before you travel.

In Kopa Life your service year starts at the camp gate too. A soldier checks your call-up letter, you queue for registration, collect your khaki and jungle boots, and learn drills with your platoon. Our NYSC camp guide has the full packing list and daily routine.

LGAs in Nasarawa you can be posted to

Nasarawa State has 13 local government areas. Your posting letter names one of them, and that LGA's NYSC office is where you do monthly clearance and meet your LGI. Busier, more urban areas are outlined in green.

  • Akwanga
  • Awe
  • Doma
  • Karu
  • Keana
  • Keffi
  • Kokona
  • Lafia
  • Nasarawa
  • Nasarawa-Eggon
  • Obi
  • Toto
  • Wamba

2 of 13 are busier urban areas in the game: more corpers, longer clearance queues and a louder group chat.

Corper life in Lafia

The weather is hot, dry savanna weather with a rainy season. To get around, corpers use keke (tricycles), okada (motorbikes) and buses. In the game, food and transport prices in Nasarawa are cheaper than Lagos.

Local languages you will hear: Hausa / Yoruba / Tiv / Nupe. Expect greetings like “Sannu, corper!” and “Ẹ káàbọ̀ o!”. At the buka you will find Pounded yam & egusi, Tuwo shinkafa & miyan kuka, Suya and Masa, with Kunu to wash it down.
