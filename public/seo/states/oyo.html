The Oyo orientation camp

Corpers deployed to Oyo State report to the orientation camp at Iseyin (Government Technical College) for about three weeks of registration, kitting, swearing-in, parade, SAED lectures and evenings at the mammy market. At the end of camp, everyone gets a posting letter to a PPA in one of the state's LGAs.

Camp locations can change between batches, for example when a camp is being renovated or for security reasons. Always confirm with your call-up letter and the official NYSC portal before you travel.

In Kopa Life your service year starts at the camp gate too. A soldier checks your call-up letter, you queue for registration, collect your khaki and jungle boots, and learn drills with your platoon. Our NYSC camp guide has the full packing list and daily routine.

Corper life in Ibadan

The weather is hot and humid most of the year. To get around, corpers use keke (tricycles), okada (motorbikes) and buses. In the game, food and transport prices in Oyo are cheaper than Lagos.

Brown rooftops of Ibadan, amala at Bodija, and very long streets.

Local languages you will hear: Yoruba. Expect greetings like “Ẹ káàárọ̀ o, corper!” and “Corper, ẹ kú iṣẹ́ o!”. At the buka you will find Amala & ewedu, Ofada rice & ayamase, Ewa agoyin & bread and Puff-puff, with Zobo to wash it down.
